Bakery Care Guide

To preserve the freshness and perfect crunch of your sourdough, follow these simple steps:

  • Room Temperature (1-3 Days): Keep the bread cut-side down on a wooden board or in a paper/cloth bag. Avoid plastic bags.
  • Freezing (Up to 1 Month): Slice the bread and store it in an airtight freezer bag. Toast slices directly from frozen.
  • Reheating Instruction: Sprinkle a few drops of water on the crust and warm in the oven at 180°C for 5 minutes.
